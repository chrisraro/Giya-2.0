## Prompt used

> Premium brand-kit overview board for "giya" — a loyalty/rewards platform for Philippine food & retail SMEs. Layout: a 3x3 grid of panels on a dark charcoal presentation canvas with strong gutters, clean alignment, and generous negative space, like a senior identity studio's brand-guidelines board. Panels: (1) logo cover: minimal lowercase geometric "g" monogram mark whose negative space forms a north-east-pointing compass needle, next to the lowercase wordmark "giya"; (2) logo construction: the same mark on a circle grid with thin construction lines and a 45-degree needle axis; (3) digital application: a minimal mobile app bottom-navigation mockup with a centered golden scan button; (4) brand essence: the tagline "Every receipt counts." in large clean type on a quiet dark panel; (5) color system: swatch cards for coral #E8563F, deep teal #00696D, mango gold #F2A93B, and warm off-white neutrals; (6) typography: a Geist Sans type specimen with large "Aa" and weights 400-600; (7) physical application: a loyalty stamp card and a paper receipt mockup; (8) image direction: a warm cinematic Philippine street-food or cafe scene at dusk; (9) system detail: small points-badge chips reading "+120 pts" and a dashed-circle stamp icon. Style: premium, sparse, cinematic, intentional; disciplined coral/teal/mango accents on charcoal; very little text, no clutter, no generic startup gradients. Brand personality: warm, vibrant, guiding, trustworthy; "giya" means "guide" in Filipino.
